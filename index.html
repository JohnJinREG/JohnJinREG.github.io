About

John Jin began his nuclear engineering career at the Korea Atomic Energy Research Institute (KAERI) in 1987. His work evolved from reactor structural dynamics and seismic engineering to nuclear safety regulation, structural integrity, long-term operation and licensing.

His career includes the Canadian Nuclear Safety Commission (CNSC), the UK's Office for Nuclear Regulation (ONR), the UAE's Federal Authority for Nuclear Regulation (FANR), and AtkinsRéalis – CANDU Energy. Earlier papers were published as Choon-Eon Jin (진춘언); later work has appeared as John Jin.
